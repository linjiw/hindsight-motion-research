Foot metrics are ankle-link forward-kinematics reconstruction errors after 1.2 s, not measured foot slip or contact. Geometry uses 643 frozen candidates across four existing pairs and compares with the continuous reference, not physical ground truth. Oracle rows use original legs.

The information bill

RVQ: 2 × 7 bits × 10 Hz = 140 bit/s. body9 adds 9 × 12 × 10 = 1,080 bit/s. leg12 adds 12 × 12 × 10 = 1,440 bit/s.

All methods additionally retain 11,200 bit/s of root state. body9 + leg12 therefore uses 13,860 bit/s including root, before entry, model, and container overhead. Linear29 uses 14,680 bit/s including root, so the hybrid saves only 5.6% of these combined streams. Prediction cost and execution still matter. NPZ storage is not a packed codec.

The qualification gate

Pass native traversal checks and retain fidelity to the original reference: mean body error ≤10 cm, maximum root XY error ≤25 cm, and 200 valid control steps.

A pair qualifies only when both continuations pass all three perturbations. Smoothing also changes within-patch motion; leg replacement changes both positions and velocities.

DEPLOYED ACTOR VIEW

What the robot can know now

Measured proprioceptive history, previous actions, available scene observations, goal and optional controls. Future scene tokens must distinguish unknown space from known free space.

TRAINING-ONLY VIEW

What teaches the behavior

Future reference, privileged simulator state, teacher actions or validated motor targets, relation labels, and support masks. Source IDs and future motion codes must not leak into the causal actor.

A tracker provides motor expertise. Task expertise also needs a qualified continuation selector or planner. Teacher labels at student-visited states must be queried at that same state; unsupported recovery is not a positive imitation target.

How to reuse this work in BFM research
  1. Start with the public contracts. Inspect protocols, scoring code, and aggregate summaries. Native data generation requires separately authorized local motion and runtime assets.
  2. Choose a motor interface. Pin teacher checkpoint, joint order, action scale, history layout, and decoder schema. Revalidate targets when the controller changes.
  3. Keep the views separate. Train only on supported rows; keep privileged reference and outcomes out of actor inputs. The latest representation collection has 19,200 control rows and 16,800 supported imitation rows.
  4. Replace one component at a time. Compare a new codec under the frozen geometry and execution scorer, or a new student under the same dataset, decoder, and training budget.
  5. Test independent scene-first tasks. Group descendants by source ancestry. Success on reused motion pairs is development evidence, not held-out navigation generalization.

05 / REVISED RESEARCH ORDER

One task. Informative comparisons.

This proposed order aligns with the broader local traversal program. It does not authorize a new experiment or change historical scores.

01 Approach→02 Duck & clear→03 Recover→04 Stop & hold

Historical episodes scored moving arrival. Recovery and sustained stopping require a new task profile; they are not already validated here.

01
REFERENCE PASSED · HANDOFF NEXT

Executable baseline

Both prescribed reference routes pass the first development pilot. Next qualify continuation from matched actual states, then a causal task-aware composer.

Keep continuous/Linear29 controls; native parity and conservative crouch-with-recovery remain separate comparisons.
02
PROPOSED · REPRESENTATION

Preserve the decision

After locating a representation bottleneck, test a joint whole-body, incoming-state-conditioned continuous latent. Quantize only after useful gains.

Gate: reference fidelity, support, clearance, switching and actual incoming-state execution.
03
PROPOSED · LEARNING

Downstream utility

Fix the learner and backend. Compare output representations; separately test hindsight versus simpler scene-first data.

Gate: complete held-out tasks, clear-task retention, acquisition cost and training-seed variation.
04
PROPOSED · OBSERVATION

Perception & memory

Replace the known map with causal depth or LiDAR. Track floor, side and ceiling geometry as observations become stale or occluded.

Gate: sensor-only from-reset control, unknown-space handling and latency robustness.
05
FUTURE · EXTENSION

Agents & richer traversal

Extend the small-model probe to real motion retention and matched-state execution. Add routes, mixed obstacles and, with a new contact contract, intentional hand/arm support.

Gate: novel compositions and compatible adapters. No automatic cross-robot transfer.

When to simplify

If the learned codec only wins offline error, keep Linear29 or native tokens. If the controller cannot execute the reference, address execution support. A null data comparison favors simpler acquisition.

What “compatible” means

Versioned task, observation, chunk, motor and feedback semantics. A language agent supplies grounded intent; a VLA may predict continuous chunks. Neither requires arbitrary motion IDs to become words.
